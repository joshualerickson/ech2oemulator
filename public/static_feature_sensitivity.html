Coupled canopy sensitivity

Forcings and all non-canopy statics are fixed. Each bar is the prediction change from the same pixel’s baseline—not a causal intervention estimate.

Canopy configurations. Coupled low is VCF −1 SD and LAI −1 SD; coupled high is VCF +1 SD and LAI +1 SD. The two mixed configurations change one feature upward and the other downward. VCF and LAI are strongly correlated in training data, so read mixed configurations as model diagnostics. Bars are grouped by month, never stacked. Quartiles are global training-pixel ranges; hover to see physical bounds.

Support behind the bars

Each row is the exact support used for that month/stratum response. Mean, median, P10, and P90 are calculated over equal-weight site/date spatial means; pooled pixels document spatial coverage but do not weight the response statistic.

How to read this diagnostic

  • Positive values mean the emulator predicts a higher target than its unmodified baseline; negative values mean lower.
  • These are pixel-level perturbations, averaged over valid pixels within each sampled site/date and then summarized across samples.
  • The P10–P90 interval describes variation across contributing site/date responses. An interval spanning zero means the response is not directionally consistent across those contexts.
  • Global DEM, TPI, TWI, and deficit quartiles need not occur inside every small bbox; the support table makes that coverage explicit.
  • VCF and LAI are observationally correlated, so this supports interpretation of their shared canopy-gradient response—not attribution to one vegetation variable.
